---
name: Catalog facet landing pages
description: The durable rule for expanding ARTCOVR organic search coverage from real catalog metadata.
---

Organic-search expansion should use populated catalog facets such as supported music genres, not arbitrary keyword permutations. Each indexable facet page must expose real matching inventory, useful context, direct product links, canonical metadata, and structured data; empty or invented facets stay out of the index.

**Why:** Search relevance depends on useful differentiated content, and thin doorway pages would dilute the storefront’s quality while making the sitemap and catalog promises harder to trust.

**How to apply:** When adding a new searchable facet, derive the allowed slugs from the approved public catalog, prerender only populated values, link the pages from the existing discovery graph, and keep the page copy specific to the actual collection.
